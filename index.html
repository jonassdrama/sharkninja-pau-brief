"The SharkNinja Neighborhood" is a brand architecture of distinct personas. Each ambassador owns a lane: Beckham (global cooking), Brady (outdoor/performance USA), Hart (comedy/mass), Klum (EMEA beauty). Lane assignment is deliberate — ambassadors complement, not compete.

Café de Finca — Conflict Assessment

FACT Café de Finca is a premium specialty coffee brand (Castelldefels, founded 2009). Investors: Pau Gasol, Marc Gasol, Ter Stegen, Falcao, Ansu Fati. €2.5M raised 2024. Pau participated publicly in "Descubre tu origen" campaign (assigned Ethiopia/Sidamo origin).


The conflict: Ninja Luxe Café Pro = premium bean-to-cup espresso machine ($749). Café de Finca = sells the beans. Asking Pau to promote a machine while he is an investor/campaign face of a premium coffee brand creates brand equity tension.


The resolution: Three options: (A) Exclude Ninja coffee machines from the deal entirely — focus on cooking/outdoor/blender portfolio; (B) Carve out Café de Finca explicitly so it is not a competing brand, and avoid coffee machine content; (C) Co-brand integration — "Café de Finca beans, Ninja machine" — requires formal agreement between both brands. Note: Beckham already has the Luxe Café Pro. Pau doesn't need to duplicate that territory.

15 · Critical Issue15 · Cuestión Crítica
Territory vs. EMEA Usage — The Unresolved RiskTerritorio vs. Uso EMEA — El Riesgo Sin Resolver
The proposal states: Territory: Spain / Usage: EMEA. These two clauses are in fundamental tension and must be resolved before signing.La propuesta indica: Territorio: España / Uso: EMEA. Estas dos cláusulas están en tensión fundamental y deben resolverse antes de firmar.

The conflict

Territory = Spain means Pau's obligations and exclusivity apply in Spain. He is not required to promote SharkNinja in France or Germany.

EMEA Usage means SharkNinja can deploy all captured content — paid media, retailer.com, social, e-commerce — anywhere across Europe, Middle East, and Africa.

This means: a campaign Pau approved for Spain could run as paid media in Germany, on French retailer websites, or in a UAE in-store display — without any additional consent from Pau.

Questions that must be answered

  • Can Pau's image appear in paid media campaigns outside Spain?
  • Can retailers outside Spain (Currys UK, FNAC France, MediaMarkt Germany) use Pau's likeness?
  • Can SharkNinja EMEA local teams access and adapt the assets independently?
  • Can content appear on pan-European websites (sharkninja.eu, ninjakitchen.eu)?
  • Can Amazon.co.uk or Amazon.de list products with Pau's image?
  • Do assets remain live after the 6-month term? What is the takedown period?
  • If assets run in EMEA, does Pau's exclusivity apply in those territories too?
Advisory recommendation: The EMEA usage right in a Spain-territory deal should carry an additional fee. If SharkNinja wants Pau's assets running across EMEA as paid media, they are effectively getting a pan-European ambassador at a Spain ambassador price. Either (A) restrict usage to Spain only + SharkNinja.com global, or (B) expand the fee to reflect the EMEA usage value. Option B must be accompanied by a named territory list and a clear definition of "EMEA" (which countries exactly). INFERENCE — requires legal review

1. Prior Approval
Ambassador's prior written approval is required for: (a) all products and product categories to be featured; (b) all creative concepts, scripts, and treatments; (c) all copy, claims, product claims, and key messages; (d) all final video edits and final photographic selections; (e) all captions, hashtags, and associated social copy; (f) all paid media placements and paid social adaptations; (g) all retail executions, including product page imagery, in-store displays, promotional materials, and retailer-specific adaptations; (h) any sub-licensing of assets to third parties including retailers, media agencies, and media partners. Approval of a creative asset in one context does not constitute approval of that asset in any other context. Each new deployment context constitutes a new use requiring approval.
2. Prohibited Contexts
Without Ambassador's separate prior written consent, assets may not be used in any context that: (a) is price-led, discount-led, or clearance-adjacent; (b) implies Ambassador's personal endorsement of a sale, promotional event, or reduced-price offer; (c) is politically sensitive or potentially controversial; (d) is derogatory to Ambassador or inconsistent with Ambassador's established public reputation; (e) is inconsistent with Ambassador's stated philanthropic or advocacy positions, including but not limited to Ambassador's role as UNICEF Global Champion for Nutrition and Zero Childhood Obesity and Ambassador's role with the Gasol Foundation; (f) positions Ambassador's likeness alongside messages promoting excessive consumption, unhealthy behaviours, or content inconsistent with childhood health and wellbeing advocacy.
3. Retail Usage
Retail usage rights are limited to: named and approved retail partners as specified in Schedule [X]; approved creative formats as specified in Schedule [Y]; non-price-led, non-discount, brand-led contexts only. Retailers receiving licensed assets may not create independent adaptations, use assets in their own paid media, or deploy assets in print catalogues, promotional leaflets, price-driven POS, or clearance material without separate written consent from Ambassador.
4. Material Changes
Any material change to approved assets — including but not limited to cropping, recolouring, addition of text overlays, combination with other creative elements, or change of deployment context — constitutes a new use requiring new approval under clause 1 above.
5. AI and Synthetic Media
Company shall not use generative AI, synthetic media, digital replicas, voice cloning, face replacement, avatar technology, translation/lip-sync alteration, or any other technology to: (a) create new performances or statements by Ambassador; (b) alter Ambassador's voice, likeness, or image beyond standard post-production corrections; (c) generate content that suggests Ambassador made statements, endorsements, or appearances that did not occur. The authorisation to use existing approved assets does not authorise the creation of new performances or appearances by Ambassador through any technological means without separate express written consent.
6. Sub-licensing
Retailers, agencies, media partners, and any other third parties receiving licensed assets from Company may use those assets only within the scope of an approved campaign as specified in Schedule [Z]. Third parties may not independently create new uses, adaptations, or deployments of Ambassador's likeness outside that approved scope.
7. Post-Term Takedown
Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Company shall remove all Ambassador assets from: (a) paid media within [30] days; (b) owned digital channels and websites within [60] days; (c) physical retail and in-store materials within [90] days. Ambassador's likeness shall not be used in any new creative production after the term. Archive use within internal company materials for non-commercial purposes may continue indefinitely.
8. Approval Timelines
Ambassador's team shall provide approval or reasoned feedback within [5] business days of receiving any material for approval. Company shall provide a revised version within [5] business days of receiving feedback. If Ambassador's team does not respond within [5] business days, this does not constitute deemed approval; Company shall resubmit.
